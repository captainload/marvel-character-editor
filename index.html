💥 Secondary Combat Conditions & Resolution Tables

💨 Slam Result Resolution

When an attack inflicts a Slam result, compare attacker's Strength/Damage against the target's Strength/Endurance:

Relative Power Result on Target
Attacker ≤ Target No Effect or knocked back 1 area
Attacker > Target by 1 Rank Knocked back 1-2 areas + knocked prone
Attacker > Target by 2+ Ranks Grand Slam: Knocked back 1-10 (1d10) areas + Stun check!
Collision with Barrier Target takes damage equal to lesser of wall Material rank or 10 damage per remaining area.

💫 Stun FEAT Resolution

When an attack inflicts a Stun result, the target immediately makes an Endurance FEAT:

Endurance FEAT Color Stun Outcome
White (Failure) Knocked Out: Target is unconscious for 1-10 (1d10) rounds!
Green Stunned: Loses next action; drops guard.
Yellow Shaken: -1CS to all actions for 1 round.
Red No Effect: Shakes off the concussive blow immediately.

☠️ Kill FEAT Resolution (Lethal Edged / Shooting / Energy)

When an attack achieves a Kill result, the target immediately makes an Endurance FEAT:

Endurance FEAT Color Lethal Outcome
White (Failure) Mortal Wound: Health reduced to 0; hero begins Dying!
Green Severe Injury: Broken bone or severed muscle; double damage or -2CS.
Yellow Blood Loss: Normal damage + 1-10 ongoing damage each turn.
Red Flesh Wound: Normal attack damage only; no lethal effect.

⚡ Multi-Attacks, Initiative & Surprise

  • Multiple Attacks in 1 Round: A hero may attempt multiple strikes by rolling Fighting (melee) or Agility (ranged) before acting:
    • 2 attacks: Requires a Yellow FEAT.
    • 3 attacks: Requires a Red FEAT.
    • Failure (White): Fumbles and loses all attacks this turn!
  • Team Initiative: At the start of each round, each team rolls 1d10. High roll acts first.
  • Surprise: Roll Intuition vs foe's Intuition. Surprised characters cannot Dodge, Evade, or Block on round 1.

📐 Column Shifts (CS):
• +1CS: Shifts one column to the right (e.g. Typical → Good).
• -1CS: Shifts one column to the left (e.g. Typical → Poor).
• Absolute bounds: Shifts cannot drop below Shift 0 or exceed Beyond.
⏱️ Universal Time & Scale Constants:
• 1 Turn = 6 Seconds (10 Turns = 1 Minute of combat).
• 1 City Area = 132 Feet (44 yards) (approx. 40 meters / typical Manhattan city block width).
• Speed Conversion: 1 Area per Turn = 15 Miles Per Hour (MPH) = 22 Feet per Second (fps).

🪂 Specialized Movement, Maneuvers & Falling Damage

Vertical Climbing & Diving

  • Climbing Straight Up: Moving vertically upward costs double movement (1/2 normal speed in areas).
  • Diving Straight Down: Gravity adds a free +2 areas/turn bonus (+30 mph) to dive speed.
  • High-Speed Turns: Turning > 45° at speeds ≥ 6 areas/turn requires an Agility FEAT. Failure (White) causes overshoot or temporary stall!

❤️ Zero Health, Dying & Bleedout Mechanics

Rules for mortal combat, bleedout, resuscitation, and natural recuperation:

⚠️ The Dying Condition (Bleedout Rule):
When a character is reduced to 0 Health from lethal attacks (edged weapons, shooting, energy blasts, or lethal blunt impacts):
1. The hero immediately passes out and begins Dying.
2. Bleedout Rate: At the conclusion of each combat round, the character loses 1 point from their Endurance Rank Number (e.g. Remarkable 30 becomes 29, 28...).
3. Death: When the character's Endurance drops to Shift 0 (0 points), the character is clinically dead and cannot be revived by normal means.

🚑 First Aid & Medical Stabilization

  • Administering First Aid: Any conscious ally can spend 1 entire action administering First Aid to a dying character.
    • Normal hero: Requires a Reason FEAT (or Intuition FEAT).
    • First Aid Talent: Automatic success + hero stabilizes instantly.
    • Medicine / Surgery Talent: Automatic stabilization + immediately restores 1-10 Health points.
  • Halting Bleedout: Once successfully stabilized, Endurance point loss stops immediately. The character remains unconscious at 0 Health until treated.

🛌 Natural Healing & Recovery Rates

  • Health Recovery: A resting character recovers Health equal to their Endurance Rank Number for every 24 hours of rest.
    • Regeneration Power: Recovers Health every round or minute according to power rank.
  • Endurance Recovery: Lost Endurance points from bleedout recover at a rate of 1 Endurance point per day of complete bed rest.
  • Passing Out (Non-Lethal Blunt Damage): When reduced to 0 Health from punches, kicks, or blunt weapons:
    • Character does NOT bleed out.
    • Remains unconscious for 1-10 (1d10) turns.
    • Awakens automatically with 1 Health.
